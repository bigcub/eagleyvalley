## Validation and playability

The repository cleanup on 4 October passed fresh build/type/lint/format checks, journey, locations, Hough, School Street, Valley entrance, gardens and ride. The before/after comparison confirms identical geometry, heights and collisions. World v0.3.95 is unchanged by the cleanup.

The game supports the full drive from Blackburn Road to Bridge Mill court, car switching, passage walking, bird mode, location feedback and reset. There is no traffic, pedestrian simulation or interior exploration.

The v0.3.95 checks passed build, TypeScript, oxlint and whitespace. Full journey55/55, vehicle switch, frontage walk/reset, School House drive55/55 and pavement19/19 passed. School Street pavement loop42/42 and drive/reverse4/4 each passed with no browser errors. The School Street drive starts on its eastern carriageway through guarded test setup because the existing Hough filtered-link bollards prevent a direct car approach; subsequent movement uses normal inputs and collisions.

Main-route ride46/46 retained maximum height jerk0.0196m and pitch step0.26°; Hough jerk0.0074m and pitch0.16° were unchanged. That route does not traverse School Street. Its separate drive/reverse and walk checks are documented in the audit. Regression height changes were confined to School Street and collision changes to its new wall. Two street directions, western overhead and skill controls/state captures were inspected with visible WORLD0.3.95.

Most buildings outside the typed streets remain generic placeholders. Mapped centrelines and EA terrain provide the base, while many widths, wall heights, kerb radii and building details are estimated. Preserve smooth driving and road-level pavement movement. The v0.4.0 raised road network was reverted after it rocked the car and slowed loading. No road signs, double yellow lines or objective box.
