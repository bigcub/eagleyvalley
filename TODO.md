# Eagley accuracy and detail checklist

Working plan, 7 September 2026. The aim is a recognisable reconstruction of the area, from the Blackburn Road entrance down Eagley Way, around Threadfold Way and into Bridge Mill, with convincing surroundings when driving or walking.

This is a work backlog, not a record of a completed survey. Previous Street View checks covered parts of Eagley Way, its entrance, the mill court entrance and parts of Threadfold Way. The entire area has not been reviewed systematically. Existing models are provisional even where they already contain detail.

## What counts as accurate

- [ ] Match the layout and scale before adding small decorative objects.
- [ ] Check every visible building individually. Stop using a generic house as the final model for a distinctive building.
- [ ] Distinguish verified details from estimates and concealed features.
- [ ] Match the present-day area using current user observations where older imagery differs.
- [ ] Preserve the user's confirmed corrections: Bridge Mill is the same buff stone at the front and rear; the passage wall has a sloping profile that must match the road above; the former engine/boiler house is a separate lower side building.
- [ ] Verify each finished section from road level, walking height and the reverse direction.
- [ ] Keep the complete driving and walking journey usable throughout the work.

## How to survey and finish each section

Use this process for every numbered section below. Finish a coherent section before scattering detail elsewhere. Continue independent work when a particular feature needs a user photo.

- [ ] Mark the section's actual start and finish on a reference map. The labels below are work areas, not claims about exact street boundaries.
- [ ] Open Google Street View and travel through the section continuously, looking at both sides and behind, rather than sampling one panorama.
- [ ] Inspect every junction, entrance, change in gradient, wall transition and distinctive building.
- [ ] Stop at roughly 10–20 metre intervals where detail changes. Use closer intervals around entrances and complex structures.
- [ ] Record panorama links, capture dates, viewing directions and what each view establishes.
- [ ] Follow available side streets and return views to identify side elevations and hidden connections.
- [ ] Record missing coverage explicitly. Private courts, gardens and concealed elevations must not be described as Street View verified.
- [ ] Compare overhead footprints and terrain data with street-level evidence. Do not infer heights from map outlines alone.
- [ ] Create a numbered inventory of buildings, boundary segments, entrances, trees and street furniture.
- [ ] Record discrepancies against the current game before editing it.
- [ ] Correct ground levels, footprints and roof forms first; then openings, boundaries, materials and planting.
- [ ] Capture matching in-game viewpoints and compare them with the references.
- [ ] Run the relevant driving/walking checks and inspect browser errors.
- [ ] Update this checklist, the section record and `progress.md`. A passing build alone does not complete a section.

Each section record should contain its boundaries, reference dates/links, asset IDs, observed features, current discrepancies, implementation status, missing photos, comparison screenshots and unresolved estimates. Use the states **not surveyed**, **surveyed**, **modelled**, **visually checked**, and **needs photos** separately.
